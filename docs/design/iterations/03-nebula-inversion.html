Bold — the FTL read

Full commitment. Dark mode has real pink bleeding in at the corners; light mode is unmistakably inside the dust with violet space at the edges. Most distinctive, most memorable, and nothing else looks like it. Cost: the violet vignette occupies a lot of a wide monitor, and strong chroma behind content is where reading comfort gets tested.

Restrained — same idea, lower chroma

The identical inversion, dialled back: the dust is still rose rather than white, the edges still go violet, but content sits on a calm surface. Holds up on a 32-inch monitor and through a long session. Matches the "restrained atmosphere" you already chose — the drama lives at the edges, not under the text.

Where I would push back, gently

Light mode still has a job: being usable in daylight, and being what someone reaches for when the dark theme is too intense. Rose dust does that — it is genuinely light. A dark violet vignette around it is fine and beautiful. But if the violet creeps too far inward, "light mode" stops being lighter and the setting loses its purpose. The restrained option keeps that promise; the bold one is closer to the edge.

Worth knowing: the pink is not decoration. H-alpha emission from ionised hydrogen is why Orion and Carina photograph pink. Using it as the platform's dust is astronomically honest, which is a nicer story than "we liked purple".
