Two things the light column exposes

1. The accent has to change value, not just mode. The gold and teal that glow on black are too pale on white — they are darkened in the light column, which is why the light accents look like different colours. That is correct, and it is a rule the tokens must encode.

2. Pale fursonas need a ring on light, dark ones need it on dark. Look at the white dot in each light column and the black dot in each dark column — without an outline they vanish into the field. Whichever palette wins, avatars need a subtle contrast ring that flips with the mode.
