3 · Clouds, stars, and slow drift

Adds a sparse star field and a very slow drift — watch it for ten seconds. Most alive, most memorable. Costs: the filter repaints while animating, which is real battery on a laptop, and it must stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

The honest tension

You chose restrained atmosphere earlier, and levels 2 and 3 push past that. That is fine if it is deliberate — but the rule we set still holds: the fursona has to be the brightest thing on screen. Look at the six dots in level 3 versus level 1. In 3 they are competing with the background rather than sitting on it. That is the cost of "more".

Also worth weighing: this is a login page. It should appear instantly for someone who just wants to get in. Level 2 is free in that sense — no images, no JavaScript, the browser generates the noise. Level 3 is the only one that costs ongoing CPU.

A middle path exists and may be the real answer: level 2 everywhere, level 3 only on the actor picker — the one screen that is about choosing your star, where drama is the point and you are not in a hurry.
