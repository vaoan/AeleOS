Three nebula directions

Each shows the real surface that matters — the sign-in card — plus how six very different fursona colours sit against it. The field has to make all of them pop, not just the ones that happen to match.

A · Carina — cool field, warm star

Indigo-violet space with a sodium-gold accent. The one direction that is not a purple gradient, so it dodges the AI-startup cliché entirely. Warm accent reads as welcoming; gold on indigo passes contrast easily. Furthest from Libra's pink.

B · Orion — violet to magenta

Closest to what you described: violets, deep blues, magenta. Unmistakably "space dust", and the most emotionally warm of the three. The risk is real though — this is the palette every AI product currently uses, and the magenta sits nearest Libra's pink, so the two apps may read as similar.

C · Deep field — teal and magenta

Near-black blue with a dusty teal primary and magenta held back for rare moments. Real nebula photography is full of these unexpected pairings. Quietest and most "infrastructure" of the three — the field recedes furthest, so avatars carry all the colour.

What to look at

Ignore which is prettiest in isolation. Look at the six fursona dots in each — a neon-green dragon, a hot-pink bunny, a white wolf, a black cat. The right field is the one where none of them looks wrong. That is the job.
